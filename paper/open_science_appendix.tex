\section{Open Science}
\label{app:open-science}

The anonymous review artifact is available at
\url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/RAIN-8C63/}. The link does not require authentication or
use access tracking and will remain available through the shepherd-approval
deadline. The artifact contains the RAIN implementation, protocol and privacy
tests, locked experiment configurations, attack and baseline integrations,
external-baseline provenance tools, and machine-readable experiment and result
manifests. The reviewer quick start and the mapping from paper experiments to
commands are provided in the top-level README and in
\texttt{docs/OPEN\_SCIENCE.md}.

CIFAR-10 is downloaded by the artifact through torchvision. FEMNIST is prepared
with the included downloader/converter or supplied as standard LEAF shards.
Tiny-ImageNet is not redistributed because access is governed by its upstream
distributor; the artifact documents the required directory layout and validates
the data before training. Camel and FLGuard are not copied into our repository;
their public repositories, exact commits, installation commands, and execution
provenance wrappers are included. Any unavailable or incomplete item is marked
explicitly in \texttt{paper/artifact\_inventory.json} and
\texttt{paper/results\_manifest.json}; source availability is never represented
as a completed experimental result.

If the paper is accepted, all shareable artifacts, completed raw measurements,
resolved configurations, and checksums will be published at a stable,
non-anonymous archival URL, which will replace the review link in the
camera-ready version.
